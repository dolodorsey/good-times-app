Privacy Policy

GOOD TIMES is a city discovery and planning service for nightlife, dining, events, culture, and experiences. This policy explains the information used to operate and personalize GOOD TIMES.

Effective August 8, 2026

Information we collect

Depending on how you use GOOD TIMES, we may process account information such as your email address and profile details; city and preference choices; saved events and venues; itineraries and concierge requests; booking or loyalty activity created through GOOD TIMES; push-notification tokens; and product interactions such as viewed, saved, ticket-clicked, reservation-clicked, and recommendation activity.

Location

If you grant location permission on a supported device, GOOD TIMES may use your location to help show nearby events and venues. You can decline location access and continue using city selection and search features.

How we use information

  • Operate authentication, profiles, saved items, plans, concierge features, and customer support.
  • Rank and personalize events, venues, and recommendations based on your selected city, preferences, and activity.
  • Maintain service reliability, security, fraud prevention, diagnostics, and performance.
  • Send notifications you have enabled about relevant GOOD TIMES activity.
  • Measure product usage so we can improve GOOD TIMES.

Service providers and external destinations

GOOD TIMES uses infrastructure and service providers to host the app, store account data, and operate product features. When you choose an external ticket, reservation, directions, venue, or other third-party link, that destination operates under its own privacy practices.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell GOOD TIMES customer personal information for money. We may share information with service providers working on our behalf, when you direct us to an external service, or when disclosure is reasonably necessary for security, legal compliance, or protection of rights.

Retention

We retain account and product information for as long as reasonably necessary to operate GOOD TIMES, provide requested services, maintain security and records, and meet applicable legal obligations. When you delete your account, GOOD TIMES removes the account and associated customer data handled by the app, except information we are legally required to retain.

Your choices

You can change device permissions in your operating-system settings, stop using external links, sign out at any time, and delete your GOOD TIMES account from the in-app Account center. Account deletion is permanent and removes your profile, saves, plans, personalization history, and account access.

Account deletion: Open GOOD TIMES → Account → Delete account permanently. For privacy or support questions, visit Support & Privacy Choices.

Children

GOOD TIMES is designed for adults and is not intended for children under 13. Some nightlife content may be intended only for users of legal age in the applicable location.

Security

We use technical and organizational measures intended to protect GOOD TIMES account data. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Changes

We may update this policy as GOOD TIMES evolves. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are published.
