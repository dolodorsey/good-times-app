Support & Privacy Choices

Help with your GOOD TIMES account, city experience, saved plans, privacy choices, and account deletion.

Support page updated August 8, 2026

Account access

If sign-in fails, use the password-reset option from the GOOD TIMES sign-in screen. Never send us your password.

City or content refresh

Confirm your selected city, refresh GOOD TIMES, then retry the event, venue, map, or planner surface.

Tickets & reservations

GOOD TIMES may route you to an external ticketing, reservation, venue, or directions provider. Final purchase and fulfillment are handled by that destination.

Location choices

You can allow or deny device location access in your operating-system settings. City selection and search remain available without precise location.

Delete your GOOD TIMES account

Signed-in users can permanently delete their account directly inside GOOD TIMES. Open Account, choose Delete account permanently, and confirm. The app removes the GOOD TIMES profile and associated customer data handled by the service, including saves, plans, personalization history, and account access.

If you cannot access the app: email hello@thegoodtimesworldwide.com from the email address associated with your GOOD TIMES account and state that you need account-deletion assistance. Do not include your password, payment card number, or other authentication secrets.

Privacy choices

  • Use the in-app Account center to sign out or delete your account.
  • Manage location and notification permissions from your device settings.
  • Review how GOOD TIMES handles customer information in the Privacy Policy.
  • Contact support for questions about account data or privacy.

What to include in a support request

Include the email address on the GOOD TIMES account, the city you selected, the feature involved, and a short description of what happened. Screenshots are useful when available. Never send passwords, one-time codes, private keys, or full payment-card details.
