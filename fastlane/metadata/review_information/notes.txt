GOOD TIMES is an account-based city discovery and planning app. After signing in, the primary customer surfaces are Now, Dates, Build, Plans, Explore, and Vault. Location permission is optional; reviewers can select a city manually. Ticket, reservation, directions, and venue actions may open third-party destinations. Privacy Policy and Support & Privacy Choices are available from the in-app Account center. Permanent account deletion is available from Account → Delete account permanently and requires confirmation. Review credentials should be entered in App Store Connect before submission; no review password is stored in this public repository.
